name: SEO title suffix
description: Site-wide title suffix convention for public and internal pages.
---

Every page title should end with `/goldantelopeasia.com`, with no space between the slash and domain.

**Why:** the user requested this as a site-wide title convention.

**How to apply:** keep the suffix consistent in server-rendered `<title>` tags, JavaScript title overrides, social title metadata, and generated listing titles.
